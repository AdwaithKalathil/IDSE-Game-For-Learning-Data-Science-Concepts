How to play

Your objective: choose the degree with the lowest unseen-data error.

  1. Move the degree slider from 1 to 10. Watch how the curve changes and how training MSE responds.
  2. Before the test results are shown, choose the degree you think will generalize best and lock it in.
  3. Compare your guess with the degree that actually has the lowest held-out test MSE.
  4. After the reveal, move the slider again. Both errors and the score update, so you can investigate the trade-off.
Look for the trap: a very flexible curve can pass close to nearly every training point yet perform poorly on new observations.
